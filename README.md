This project was developed as a sample application of concepts learned through a PostgreSQL course. The idea was inspired by a recent family business venture exploring the rental car industry here in Asheville, North Carolina. With an established market dominated by large competitors and seasonal fluctuations in tourism, one potential advantage for a smaller rental operation is maintaining low overhead while making efficient use of its fleet. Asheville's tourism industry is also still recovering from the effects of Hurricane Helene, making inventory management and cost control particularly important. Rather than relying entirely on third-party operational software and recurring service fees, this project explores how an in-house database solution could support day-to-day operations and future business decisions. The database is designed to manage the operational side of a rental car business by centralizing information about vehicles, customers, rental transactions, payments, additional charges, maintenance, and vehicle locations. 

Potential Future Improvements 
  1. Establish mileage and vehicle age thresholds for replacement to help limit unexpected maintenance expenses.
  2. Incorporate an HTML-based interface that allows employees to enter information without interacting directly with the database.
  3. Include more detailed operating expenses and clearly defined cost variables after consulting with stakeholders.
  4. Integrate generative AI and visualization platforms such as Power BI or Sigma to create a clear picture of vehicle usage and overall business profitability.

Ultimately, this project serves as a foundation for a more comprehensive fleet management solution, with an emphasis on business intelligence, operational efficiency, and financial reporting.
